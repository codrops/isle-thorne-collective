Many of our designs begin far from the drawing table, in the quiet observation of the natural world. A river stone, worn smooth by centuries of flowing water, inspired the soft profile of a coffee table base. The curve of a branch, bent gracefully by the wind, lives on in the sweeping back of the Crescent Edge Armchair. And the Lunar Glow Lamps take their name, and a little of their light, from the serene glow of the moon itself.

We never seek to imitate these forms. Instead, we study what makes them feel so calm and complete: a single, unbroken line, a weight that rests gently close to the ground, a surface that invites the touch of a hand. These quiet principles guide each of our sketches, and they remain with every piece as it moves from the first drawing to the final finish in our workshop.

> Nature rarely uses a straight line, and almost never a sharp corner.

Colour follows the same philosophy. Our leathers echo the soft greens of moss and sage, the warm browns of bark and earth, and the deep reds of autumn leaves. Our glass is clear, smoked or delicately tinted with green, like water flowing over stone, while our metals are brushed and polished to catch the changing light of the day in the most subtle ways.

The result, we hope, is furniture that feels at home in a room the way a stone feels at home in a stream: natural, effortless, as if it had always been there. Each piece is an invitation to slow down, to reconnect with the textures and rhythms of the natural world, and to bring a sense of harmony and tranquility into the comfort of your own home.
